# Consumer Loan Policy LP-2025-v1

## 1.1 Purpose and Scope
This synthetic policy document defines automated and manual underwriting criteria for personal consumer loan applications evaluated between `2025-01-01` and `2025-06-30`. It supports audit and explanation of recorded loan decisions.

## 2.1 Eligibility Criteria
Applicants must be of legal borrowing age and apply in the configured regional currency. Protected demographic attributes (including gender, ethnicity, religion, and postcode) are strictly excluded from scoring and decision rules.

## 3.1 Affordability Assessment
Affordability is evaluated using the applicant's verified monthly income and existing monthly debt service obligations captured at decision time.

## 3.2 Debt-to-income limit
The maximum allowable debt-to-income ratio (`dti_ratio`) is `0.45` (`45%`), calculated as `existing_debt_service / monthly_income`. Applications with `dti_ratio > 0.45` exceed the affordability threshold and are declined (`DECLINE`).

## 3.3 Minimum credit score
The minimum required credit bureau score (`credit_score`) for automated approval is `600` (on a `300` to `900` scale). Applications with `credit_score < 600` do not qualify for automated approval and are declined (`DECLINE`) or referred (`REFER`) according to risk score bands.

## 4.1 Required documents
All applications under `LP-2025-v1` must provide the following required documents:
- `income proof`

## 5.1 Manual review
Applications with intermediate model risk scores or borderline credit indicators that do not trigger an immediate decline are routed to `REFER` for manual underwriter review. Any human override of an automated outcome is recorded as a separate append-only row with a mandatory written justification.
